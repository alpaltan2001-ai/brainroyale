Brain Royale

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Brain Royale is a daily brain game you play with friends. It is made by Alp Altan, an independent developer in Turkey ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores about you, why, who can see it and how to delete it. Questions: alpaltan2001@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account: when you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a sign-in id and the email address they share with us (Apple lets you hide your real address). We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Your player: the username and animal avatar you choose, and your country, taken once from your phone's region setting.
  • Your games: each game you play: the day, the game, your score and a short log of your answers and their timing. The log lets us check that results are fair.
  • Your groups: the groups you create or join, their names and emojis, your reactions to friends' scores, chat messages you send, players you block and reports you send.
  • Notifications: a push token for your phone, so we can send the notifications you switch on, and your notification choices.
  • Rewarded ads: a note that you watched an ad to unlock an extra try that day.

We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we never sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your username and avatar.
  • Members of your groups can see your scores in that group (once they've played that day themselves), your reactions, the messages you send in the group chat and your season standings and badges.
  • Your country and the date you joined are shown only to you.

Ads

You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad for one extra try in a group. These ads come from Google AdMob. Google may use device information such as your IP address and, only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks, your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. If you don't allow tracking, the ads aren't personalized. You can change this any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the game's data and runs sign-in (servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Expo delivers push notifications to your phone.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in.
  • Google AdMob provides the rewarded ads.

They process data only to provide these services, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, player, games, group memberships, reactions, chat messages and push tokens from our servers. Two things stay: past season standings of your groups keep the username and avatar you had then, and reports you sent stay (without your account) so we can review them.

Children

Brain Royale is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by writing to alpaltan2001@gmail.com. Your username, avatar and notification choices can also be changed in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
