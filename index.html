Brain Royale

A daily brain game you play with friends.

Coming soon to the App Store · now in testing

One short game a day

Every day your group gets one mini game of 1–2 minutes. The category changes daily:

Memory Focus Logic Speed Numerical

Play with your friends

Make a group, invite friends with a code and compete on a monthly leaderboard. Win medals and react to each other's scores.

Find your strengths

Your scores become Brain Points. Your profile shows how you do in each category, so you can see who has the best memory or the fastest fingers.
